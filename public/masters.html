Configure your inventory parameters and seed the initial catalog. Seeding adds over 300 spare parts, lubricants, filters, fluids, service consumables, and fast-moving parts tailored for a Mahindra automobile workshop.

Seeding Action: Automatically sets up opening stocks (10 for standard items, 50 for lubricants, 100 for service consumables) and associates them with active suppliers like Garg Traders or Hari Om Traders. This action cannot be undone and runs once.
